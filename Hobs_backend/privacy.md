# Privacy Policy — HoBS

**Last updated:** July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HoBS ("we," "us," "our," "the Service") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when hotels and their guests use our WhatsApp-based booking platform.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at **[your support email]**.

---

## 1. Who This Policy Applies To

This policy covers three categories of people:

- **Guests** — individuals who message a hotel's WhatsApp number to ask about rooms or make a booking.
- **Hotel Staff** — individuals authorized by a hotel to report booking updates via WhatsApp or to access the HoBS dashboard.
- **Hotel Owners/Administrators** — individuals who register a hotel on HoBS and manage its dashboard, pricing, and staff.

## 2. Information We Collect

### From Guests
- WhatsApp phone number
- Name, as provided during a booking
- Booking details: dates, room type, amount paid
- Message content sent to the hotel's WhatsApp number, for the purpose of answering questions and completing bookings

### From Hotel Staff
- WhatsApp phone number
- Name and role (e.g. receptionist, manager)
- Email address (for staff with dashboard or permission-management access)
- Messages sent to update room/booking status
- Login credentials (stored as a securely hashed password, never in plain text)

### From Hotel Owners/Administrators
- Business name, contact details
- Payment settlement details (bank account information, processed through our payment partner — see Section 5)
- Dashboard account credentials

### Automatically Collected
- Timestamps of messages and actions
- Basic technical metadata associated with WhatsApp message delivery (via Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform)

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information collected to:

- Answer guest questions about room availability and pricing
- Process and confirm bookings
- Facilitate payment collection on behalf of hotels
- Allow hotel staff to report and update room/booking status
- Send booking confirmations and check-out reminders to guests
- Maintain an audit trail of staff-driven changes, for accountability and dispute resolution
- Notify hotel managers of significant account activity (e.g. a cancelled paid booking)
- Improve the reliability and accuracy of our automated messaging system
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

We do **not** sell personal information to third parties, and we do not use guest or staff data for advertising.

## 4. WhatsApp Business Platform

HoBS operates using Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. When you message a hotel's WhatsApp number, your message is transmitted through Meta's infrastructure in addition to ours. Meta's own privacy practices, described at [https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/business-policy](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/business-policy), apply to that transmission independently of this policy.

## 5. Payment Processing

Payments made through HoBS are processed by a third-party payment provider. We do not store full payment card details on our servers. Transaction references and payment status are retained for booking and accounting purposes.

## 6. Data Sharing

We share information only in the following circumstances:

- **With the relevant hotel** — a guest's booking and contact details are shared with the specific hotel they are booking with, so that hotel can fulfill the reservation.
- **With service providers** — including our payment processor, messaging infrastructure (Meta), and AI/language-processing providers used to interpret messages, solely to operate the Service.
- **For legal reasons** — if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of HoBS, our users, or the public.

We do not share guest data between unrelated hotels.

## 7. Data Retention

- Booking records are retained for as long as necessary for accounting, legal, and dispute-resolution purposes.
- Staff action logs are retained indefinitely as an audit trail and are not deleted, even when an action is later reverted, to preserve the integrity of the record.
- Conversation state (e.g. an in-progress booking) is retained only for the duration needed to complete that interaction.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- Request access to the personal information we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your information, subject to our legal and legitimate business retention needs (e.g. financial records, audit logs)
- Object to certain uses of your information

To exercise these rights, contact **[your support email]**.

## 9. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect information, including encrypted connections, hashed passwords, and access controls limiting who can view guest and financial data. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from individuals under the age of 18.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
